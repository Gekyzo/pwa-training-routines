# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

- Routines are here: make your days in `Routines`, tap **Start** and log each set in `Workout`. Each new set copies the values of the last time, and a done set starts the rest timer.
- `Exercises` shows your records and the history of each exercise. **Add common exercises** gives you 29 to start with.
- Pick kg or lb and the default rest in Settings. **Export CSV** saves your sets for a spreadsheet.
- Signed in with Google, your data syncs between your devices. Offline, the changes wait and go later.
- What's new shows the names of buttons and settings in bold, with no asterisks.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-30

- First version of Routines, with its own icon. It works offline, installs on Android and has light and dark themes in English and Spanish. Sign in with Google, or use it with no account. **Delete all data** in Settings starts the app as new. Your workout routines come next.
